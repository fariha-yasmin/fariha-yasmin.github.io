## Overview

This project involved calculating the electronic band structure and density of states (DOS) of a perovskite material using Density Functional Theory (DFT), implemented through **Quantum ESPRESSO**.

## Methodology

- Structural relaxation using variable-cell optimization
- Self-consistent field (SCF) calculation to obtain the converged charge density
- Non-self-consistent field (NSCF) calculation along high-symmetry k-points
- Band structure and DOS post-processing

## Tools Used

- Quantum ESPRESSO (`pw.x`, `bands.x`, `dos.x`)
- Pseudopotentials from the SSSP library
