## Research Focus

My research interests center on **computational materials science**, using **Density Functional Theory (DFT)** to study the electronic and optical properties of materials — particularly **perovskite materials**.

## Methodology

I primarily work with **Quantum ESPRESSO**, an open-source suite for electronic-structure calculations and materials modeling based on DFT, plane waves, and pseudopotentials.

My typical workflow involves:

- Structural relaxation and geometry optimization
- Electronic band structure and density of states (DOS) calculations
- Optical property analysis (absorption, dielectric response)
- Convergence testing and pseudopotential validation

## Current Interests

- Electronic structure of perovskite materials
- Optical properties from first-principles calculations
- Method development and computational workflow efficiency
